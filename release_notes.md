# 🇵🇰 Pak Android Bloatware Remover v1.0.0

Welcome to the official **v1.0.0** release of **Pak Android Bloatware Remover** — a modern, lightweight, and safe open-source Android debloater and direct app installer for Windows.

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Key Features

### 🟢 1. Smart Bloatware Removal
- **Human-Friendly App Names**: Instantly translates confusing package IDs (e.g. `com.sec.android.app.sbrowser`) to real names like *Samsung Internet*, *Facebook Services*, *Google Drive*.
- **3-Tier Safety System**:
  - 🟢 **Safe**: Bloatware, adware, telemetry, and pre-installed partner apps.
  - 🟡 **Optional**: User apps like Chrome, Gmail, Samsung Notes, Calculator.
  - 🔴 **System Core**: Critical Android OS services protected with warning confirmations.
- **⚡ 1-Click Safe Bloatware Cleaning**: Auto-selects and uninstalls known bloatware with a single click.

### 🔄 2. 1-Click App Restoration
- View all previously uninstalled packages for User 0 and restore them back onto your phone anytime with zero data loss.

### 🔍 3. App Search & Direct Phone Installer (No Google Account Required!)
- Search for any Android app (e.g. *WhatsApp, Instagram, CapCut, Spotify, Subway Surfers, com.wt.apkinfo*).
- Inspect the official Google Play Store page with the **`🌐 Play Store`** button.
- Click **`⬇️ Download & Install to Phone`** to download and install directly via ADB without logging into any Google account on your PC or phone.
- **Drag & Drop Local APKs**: Drag any `.apk` file into the window to install immediately.

### 📶 4. Wireless ADB & Power Utilities
- Connect to your phone over Wi-Fi without needing a USB cable.
- Quick buttons to reboot normally, to Recovery mode, or to Bootloader / Fastboot.

## 🚀 Quick Setup Guide

1. **Enable Developer Options**: Go to phone `Settings` ➔ `About Phone` ➔ Tap **`Build Number`** 7 times.
2. **Enable USB Debugging**: Go to `Settings` ➔ `Developer Options` ➔ Turn **`USB Debugging`** ON.
3. **Connect Phone**: Plug your phone into PC via USB and tap **`Allow`** on your phone screen.
4. **Launch App**: Open *Pak Android Bloatware Remover*, check apps, and click **`Uninstall Selected`**!

## 🛡️ Windows SmartScreen & Security Note

> [!NOTE]
> When running the installer on a new computer, Windows SmartScreen may show a blue prompt *"Windows protected your PC"*. 
> **To proceed**: Click **`More info`** ➔ **`Run anyway`**.
> This is normal for new open-source software that is not signed with expensive enterprise certificates. The software is 100% clean and open-source.
